The style guide

Start with the basics

Choose comfort first. Shapewear should fit smoothly and allow you to breathe and move comfortably.

♡

How to choose shapewear

Check the size chart, fabric, coverage, and return policy. Avoid sizing down if it makes the garment uncomfortable.

✦

Shorts or bodysuit?

Shaping shorts can work under skirts and dresses; bodysuits offer a one-piece option. Choose based on your outfit and comfort.

☼

Care for your garments

Follow the product care label. Gentle washing and air-drying can help many stretch fabrics last longer.
